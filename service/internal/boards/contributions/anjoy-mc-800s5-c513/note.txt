MC-800S5: Anjoy 38x38 two-board IP camera module with a SigmaStar SSC378QE
  SoC and a Sony IMX415 sensor.

Chip and memory
- Tino gives the SoC as SSC378QE, "378QE (not DE)", and repeated this when
  listing SSC378 modules: "378DE in T3, and 378QE in MC800S5".
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/75138, https://t.me/c/1809358416/217580,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/289873)
- Some messages give only the family, SSC378: "MC800S5 from Anjoy is the
  same imx415 but SSC378". (https://t.me/c/1809358416/275219,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/103329)

Sensor
- Sensor is the Sony IMX415, stated repeatedly in 2025-2026 (e.g. "MC800S5 -
  ssc378qe/imx415"). (https://t.me/c/1809358416/217588,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/275219, https://t.me/c/1809358416/290764)

Flash and storage
- NOR flash: "MC800S5 - SSC378QE / IMX415 (Anjoy, 2 board, NOR)".
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/108787)

Interfaces and pinout
- Uses the "usual 38x38" form factor, two boards.
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/83140, https://t.me/c/1809358416/108787)

Firmware support (OpenIPC / majestic / stock)
- Measured in 2025-05: in the OpenIPC builder target ssc378qe-fpv, `strings
  majestic | grep -i lite` on builder/openipc/output/target/usr/bin/majestic
  returns "Lite". (https://t.me/c/1809358416/221946)

Disagreements
- SoC variant: most messages say SSC378QE, but one 2024-09 message says
  "MC800S5 (SSC378DE)". (https://t.me/c/1809358416/112010,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/108787, https://t.me/c/1809358416/217588)
- In 2024-09 Tino doubted the declared spec: "MC800S5 is declared as
  SSC378+Imx415. though i have my doubts..". Later messages again state
  SSC378QE/IMX415. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/116116,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/211071)
