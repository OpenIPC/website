MC800S (SSC338Q + IMX415): NOR flash on V3 boards, SPI NAND on older ones.

Tino Darkness's MC800S-V3 has a 16 MB NOR flash: "my MC800S-V3 has NOR too
(PY2322/ 128Mbit)" (2024-09-10, https://t.me/c/1809358416/113080). Older
MC800S V2.x boards are mostly SPI NAND: "newer MC800S models are usually
NOR. older models are NAND" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/60201). The 2021
board had a GD5F1GQ5xExxG, 1 Gbit SLC SPI NAND in WSON
(https://t.me/c/1166652144/19487). Its stock MTD table ends at 0x8000000
(128 MB), with IPL0 at 0x140000 and UBI from 0x34c0000
(https://t.me/c/1166652144/77958). On the NOR boards, the two boot-select
resistors that pick NAND are left unsoldered
(https://t.me/c/1166652144/88189).
