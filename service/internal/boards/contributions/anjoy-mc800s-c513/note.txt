MC800S: Anjoy SSC338Q + IMX415 camera module, two boards (sensor board on
  FPC), with SPI NAND on older and NOR flash on newer revisions

Chip and memory
- SoC is SigmaStar SSC338Q (https://t.me/c/1809358416/290764,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/78892, https://t.me/c/1166652144/19487)
- Measured: the boot log shows "fdt:Machine model: INFINITY6E SSC012B-S01A"
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19514)
- Single-core Dhrystone of 1695 DMIPS (SSC30KQ: 830 for comparison); Tino
  notes the numbers may be affected by over/underclocking
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/231166, https://t.me/c/1809358416/141148,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/133287)

Sensor
- Sony IMX415, 1/2.7" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/289873,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/73182, https://t.me/c/1166652144/19487)
- Tino lists it as "20fps" with the IMX415 (https://t.me/c/1166652144/77773)
- Device tree read of csi_sr0_lane_num "looks like it could be 4" (4-lane
  MIPI CSI), "at least on MC800S and K45 - same result"
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/60946)

Flash and storage
- 2021-09 unit: GD5F1GQ5xExxG, "1G-bit SLC SPI NAND in WSON"
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19487)
- Measured stock NAND MTD table: 12 partitions from IPL0 to "UBI", ending at
  0x8000000 (128 MB) (https://t.me/c/1166652144/77958)
- Flash type depends on revision: "old MC800S V2.{0,1} is mostly NAND, while
  MC800S - V3 (even with V2.1 on board) is NOR"; "newer MC800S models are
  usually NOR. older models are NAND" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/113080,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/60201, https://t.me/c/1809358416/31632)
- Tino's MC800S-V3 has NOR "PY2322/ 128Mbit" (16 MB)
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/113080)
- NAND-based units have no MXP partition (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19563)

Interfaces and pinout
- Boot media is selected by two resistors: "none soldered for NOR and 2 R's
  for NAND" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/38822,
  https://t.me/c/1166652144/88189, https://t.me/c/1809358416/38775)
- Sensor board connects over a 30-pin 0.5 mm FPC; Tino's estimate of its
  signals: I2C, 3.3/1.8/5V, MIPI 4-lane, CDS, IR-cut. The same sensor board
  is used on MC800G (SSC339), a similar one on MC-H12S (IMX577)
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/67492)
- Asked about a second UART, Tino pointed to connector D2, "used for AF
  (autofocus) on stock fw", and suggested wiring TX to D2.3 (AF_RX) and RX
  to D2.2 (AF_TX); wiring PDF at
  http://www.icamra.cn:8021/pdf/MC800S/MC800S%e6%8e%a5%e7%ba%bf%e5%9b%be.pdf
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/38847, https://t.me/c/1809358416/38864)
- Wiring diagram looks identical to MC800N2's; MC500N2's is slightly
  different (https://t.me/c/1809358416/113073)
- Per Zipray (relayed by Tino), MC800S and MC500N2 camera boards are
  compatible (https://t.me/c/1809358416/81143)
- Measured: the Taobao SD-card expansion board 739978020319 "was connected
  to MC800S V1 / NAND and the card works there"; Tino expects other such
  boards to work too, as they seem compatible with all or most Anjoy boards
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/64621)

Firmware support (OpenIPC / majestic / stock)
- Stock u-boot: "u-boot is not locked, but linux console disabled"
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19514)
- The vendor update firmware_MC800S_V0-H5_V3.0.5.5 (icamra.cn, 2021-07)
  contains only the rootfs partition, no IPL/u-boot/kernel
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19497)
- In 2025-08 Tino pointed to a Taobao listing (item 966691900762) for MC800S
  modules already flashed with OpenIPC; for orders of 100+ pre-programmed
  OpenIPC modules cost "+8 CNY" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/250195,
  https://t.me/c/1166652144/134005)

Quirks and fixes
- NAND units have "no simple way aka ‘just dump NOR, change passwd_sys,
  repack, reflash and you’re in’" (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19489)
- Measured on stock u-boot: md.w 0x1f003c00 reads 00f1
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19512)
- "MC800S/NAND (early version) cannot load UBOOT file from fat32 partition";
  workaround is kernel from SD plus rootfs over NFS
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/63519)
- Measured: "MC800S has this console spam OTB and the MC-K45 does not";
  workaround dmesg -n 1 (https://t.me/c/1809358416/65135)
- NAND-to-NOR conversion: "unsolder those two R's and replace the NAND flash
  memory chip with a NOR" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/31636)

Variants, aliases and products built on it
- Two-board design (sensor + SoC board); with the EU module it is three
  boards (https://t.me/c/1809358416/186674,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/102720)
- Sensor board joins the main board by FPC, allowing "flexible mounting"
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/51994)
- Board size 38x38 mm, same as MC800G (https://t.me/c/1809358416/71756)
- The old NAND 800S and the 800S-V3 have "slightly different SMD layout on
  the sensor board" (https://t.me/c/1809358416/113075)
- Products: an AliExpress AF+PTZ zoom camera built on "Anjoy MC800S + AF +
  PTZ board"; a Taobao 36x optical zoom camera (item 737632892561, 380-530
  RMB in 2024-04) (https://t.me/c/1809358416/30539,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/73180, https://t.me/c/1809358416/42507)
