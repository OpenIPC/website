MC-L12: older Anjoy IP camera module with a SigmaStar SSC30KQ SoC and a Sony
  IMX335 sensor; MC-L12B is the same pairing.

Chip and memory
- SoC is SSC30KQ for both MC-L12 and MC-L12B.
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/51861, https://t.me/c/1809358416/239663,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/297936)

Sensor
- Sensor is IMX335. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/29759,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/30758, https://t.me/c/1809358416/297936)
- Asked for a low-light sensor compatible with OpenIPC FPV, Tino named
  "IMX335 Anjia MC-L12" alongside "IMX347 Anjia MC-K45 (IMHO better)".
  (https://t.me/EZWBC/15381)

Firmware support (OpenIPC / majestic / stock)
- In 2023-10: "should be supported, but i'm not sure about high-fps sensor
  driver". (https://t.me/c/1809358416/29759)

Sourcing and price
- In 2024-01: "L12 is quite an old module. ~90RMB on taobao", seemingly
  replaced by the L12D. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/51867)
- L12/B ~85 RMB on Taobao in 2024-04. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/72738)
- MC-L12B 245 CNY in 2026-09. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/297936)
