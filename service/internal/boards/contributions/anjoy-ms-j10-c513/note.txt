MS-J10: Anjoy IP camera module on the SigmaStar SSC335 with 8MB NOR flash.

Chip and memory
- Measured: the IPL signature MVX4##I6b0g05c44beIPL_ puts the MS-J10 in the
  SSC335 group. (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19577)

Flash and storage
- Measured: the stock u-boot MXP partition table (IPL, IPL_CUST, MXPT, ROM,
  KEY_CUST, UBOOT, UBOOT_ENV, KERNEL, SYSTEM, DATA) ends at 0x00800000, i.e.
  8MB NOR. (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19551)

Interfaces and pinout
- Wiring diagram PDF (covers USB and debug UART pins):
  http://www.icamra.cn:8021/pdf/MS-J10/MS-J10%e6%8e%a5%e7%ba%bf%e5%9b%be.pdf
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/85714)

Firmware support (OpenIPC / majestic / stock)
- The stock firmware identifies the model by the flag file msj10.flag.
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19532)
