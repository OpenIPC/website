MC-F46: SSC30KD (I6e) camera module with a GC4653 sensor driver in stock
  firmware

Chip and memory
- SSC30KD; the IPL tag reads MVX4##I6e#g85ea583IPL_ (measured)
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19577)
- The stock SSC30KD kernel cmdline sets LX_MEM=0x7fe0000 (just under 128 MB)
  and mma_heap sz=0x4A80000, versus LX_MEM=0xffe0000 on SSC30KQ (measured)
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/74585)

Sensor
- GC4653: "there is gc4653_MIPI.ko in /opt/ch" of the stock firmware
  (measured) (https://t.me/c/1166652144/84162)

Firmware support (OpenIPC / majestic / stock)
- The stock firmware selects the model with flag files: /opt/ch/af.flag,
  /opt/ch/mcf46.flag, /opt/ch/starc.flag, /opt/ch/star.flag (measured)
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/19526)

Variants, aliases and products built on it
- The original MC-F46 and possibly the MC-F46WF, "because it's an old 2022
  board with the same SSC30KD" (https://t.me/c/1166652144/84142)

Catalogue comparison
- The catalogue lists no sensor; Tino's evidence above gives GC4653
  (https://t.me/c/1166652144/84162)
