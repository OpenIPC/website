MC-D40: Anjoy dual-sensor module on SSC30KQ with two GC2053 sensors on FFC
  camera modules

Chip and memory
- SoC is SSC30KQ (https://t.me/c/1809358416/291327,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/72319, https://t.me/c/1166652144/74863)

Sensor
- Two GC2053 sensors on one SoC (https://t.me/c/1809358416/291327,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/76993, https://t.me/c/1166652144/74863)
- Measured: stock firmware /opt/ch holds gc2053_MIPI.ko,
  gc2053_MIPI_master.ko and gc2053_MIPI_slaver.ko; the master/slave drivers
  sync the two sensors (https://t.me/c/1809358416/72319,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/74000)
- Tino: "only 1080p and not sure about 90fpc"
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/76993)

Interfaces and pinout
- "the D40 / D80 have FFC (dual) camera modules"
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/72146)
- Wiring PDF:
  http://www.icamra.cn:8021/pdf/MC-D40/MC-D40%e6%8e%a5%e7%ba%bf%e5%9b%be.pdf
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/60320)

Variants, aliases and products built on it

Disagreements
- Sensor: the catalogue lists GC2063; Tino names GC2053 and the stock
  firmware ships gc2053_MIPI.ko (https://t.me/c/1809358416/72319,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/76993)
- Later 2026 messages name the sensor less firmly ('2 x GC2093 or so', '2 x
  GC2039 or so') and the SoC as 'SSC30KD', against SSC30KQ elsewhere
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/291327, https://t.me/c/1809358416/299427)
