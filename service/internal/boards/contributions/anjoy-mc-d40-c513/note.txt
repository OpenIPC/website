MC-D40: two GC2053 sensors on one SSC30KQ.

The stock firmware's /opt/ch holds gc2053_MIPI.ko, gc2053_MIPI_master.ko
and gc2053_MIPI_slaver.ko, the master and slave drivers for two synchronised
sensors (Tino Darkness, 2024-04-10, https://t.me/c/1809358416/72319). He
gave the same in 2023: "MC-D40 also has 2 sensors, but it's based on the
SSC30KQ. GC2053 sensors though" (https://t.me/c/1166652144/74863). The
catalogue had GC2063. Some 2026 messages are less sure ("GC2093 or so"), but
the firmware listing is the measured evidence.
