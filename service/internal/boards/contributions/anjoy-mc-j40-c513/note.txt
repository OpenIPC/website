MC-J40: SSC377 (I6C) with an SC450AI sensor.

Tino Darkness: "ssc377 + SC450AI - MC-J40" (2023-10-23,
https://t.me/c/1809358416/29759). The stock firmware's sensor driver is
sc450ai_MIPI.ko (https://t.me/c/1166652144/79715). He recommends the
MC-J40[W] when a real SC450AI is wanted (https://t.me/c/1166652144/117405), unlike the MC-J30, whose firmware
loads an SP4329 driver. One 2024 message says "SC530AI can be found in J40"
(https://t.me/c/1166652144/117277), which may be another revision.

Memory: SSC377 with 64 MB of DDR2 in the package
(https://t.me/c/1166652144/73003).
