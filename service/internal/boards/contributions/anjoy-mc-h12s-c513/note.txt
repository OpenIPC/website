MC-H12S: Anjoy 12MP IP camera module on the SigmaStar SSC359G (Mercury6)
  with a Sony IMX577 sensor.

Chip and memory
- Measured: the board reports Mercury6, "Chip_ID: 0xF4" in
  /sys/class/mstar/msys/CHIP_ID (u-boot md.w 0x1f003c00 reads 00f4).
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/75106)
- SoC is "SSC359G, Mercury6 sdk family"; earlier given as "SSC359 (M6, not
  I6E)". (https://t.me/c/1809358416/291748, https://t.me/c/1166652144/80826,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/51874)
- SSC359G is dual-core, up to 1.2GHz. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/140934)

Sensor
- 12MP Sony IMX577 (Starvis2). (https://t.me/c/1166652144/80826,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/51874, https://t.me/c/1809358416/291747)
- Module performance: "SSC359G/IMX577 (4K@30 up to 1080@120)", limited by
  the SigmaStar pipeline. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/293156)

Flash and storage
- NAND flash: "MCH12S - NAND". (https://t.me/c/1166652144/77951)

Interfaces and pinout
- Sensor board connects over a 30-pin 0.5mm FFC, similar to the MC800S
  sensor board; compatibility with the MC800S was not confirmed as of
  2024-05. (https://t.me/c/1809358416/67492,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/81073, https://t.me/c/1809358416/81162)

Firmware support (OpenIPC / majestic / stock)
- Not supported by OpenIPC (2024-01; restated 2026-06): "sigmastar. just not
  pudding sdk codename. not supported by OIPC".
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/51176, https://t.me/c/1809358416/291737)

  https://t.me/c/1809358416/81073, https://t.me/c/1809358416/291747)
