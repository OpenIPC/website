MC-H12F: Anjoy 12MP IP camera module on the SigmaStar SSC338G (external DDR)
  with an OmniVision OS12D40 sensor, also sold inside 36x/40x zoom blocks.

Chip and memory
- SoC is SSC338G, "SSC338_G_ (external DDR)".
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/181307, https://t.me/c/1809358416/217386,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/287938)
- Asked how SSC338Q and 338G differ, Tino answered: "external DDR, 30fps on
  VENC / full frame, not 20, maybe something else also". RAM size not given.
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/290918)

Sensor
- Sensor is the OS12D40, 12MP, "not from sony".
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/181307, https://t.me/c/1809358416/217386,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/290259)
- At night "it really is better than IMX415".
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/287942)
- Sensor capability "11.3MP@60fps, 4K@60fps, 1080p@240fps"; on the module
  "4K@60 up to 1080p@240", limited by the SigmaStar pipeline.
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/290916, https://t.me/c/1809358416/293156)
- Tino suggested it (as "MC-12F") for low light in 2025-10.
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/264655)

Sourcing and price
- Bare module ~$60 (2026-05 and 2026-06), ~$50-60 without lens (2026-06).
  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/287942, https://t.me/c/1809358416/290259,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/291727)
- 40x optical zoom version: about $250 on Taobao (2026-06-17), $260
  (2026-06-27). (https://t.me/c/1809358416/290259,
  https://t.me/c/1809358416/291098)

Variants, aliases and products built on it

  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/217386)

Disagreements

  (https://t.me/c/1809358416/287938, https://t.me/c/1809358416/290259,
